Exception Desk

A miniature model of the core mechanic behind AI-run order-to-cash: every stuck invoice has a reason it's stuck, buried in an email thread or a mismatched field. An agent reads the context, decides a resolution path, and acts — instead of a human digging through a portal. Click an invoice, then run the agent.
