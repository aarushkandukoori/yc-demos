ScrapSort: watch a magnet sort scrap by grade

Drop a batch of shredded, mixed-purity metal into the separation column. Dial the field strength up or down and watch how a magnetic field alone splits high rare-earth-content fragments from low-grade junk — the same core trick that turns a pile of e-waste and dead motors into feedstock for a brand-new permanent magnet. Everything below is simulated with synthetic data, entirely in your browser.
