Cheapest-plan solver

A toy of the core idea behind an ICHRA decision layer: for each employee, price every plan on total expected cost (premiums plus what they will actually pay out of pocket), not sticker premium. Pick someone, change their age, household or expected care, and watch the winner flip. All plans and people below are synthetic.
