Cloudbreak — Solar Uplift Simulator

A toy model of dispatching a drone fleet to thin cloud cover over a solar array, cell by cell, and watching real-time output respond. Click any shaded cell to send a drone; darker haze means less sunlight reaching the panel underneath.
