MutaVax Lab

A toy walkthrough of the core idea behind personalized cancer vaccines for dogs: compare a tumor's DNA to healthy DNA, find the mutations unique to the cancer, rank which ones are worth training the immune system on, and watch a simulated dose take effect. All sequences and outcomes below are synthetic and generated in your browser — nothing here leaves the page.

1 Pick a patient

Every dog gets two reads: one from healthy tissue, one from the tumor biopsy. Choose a case to pull its (synthetic) sequencing data.

Select a dog to load its biopsy.

2 Compare tumor DNA to healthy DNA

Somatic mutations are the letters that changed only in the tumor. Those are the flags your dog's immune system has never seen before — and the raw material for a vaccine.

3 Score mutations for vaccine potential

Not every mutation is worth including — some sit in genome regions the immune system barely samples, others produce a protein shape (an epitope) that binds strongly to receptor cells and is easy to raise a response against. Click rows to build your vaccine payload; we've pre-selected the top scorers.

4 Vaccine payload

Each selected mutation becomes a short mRNA sequence coding for its mutant peptide, so the immune system can be shown the exact shape to look for.

5 Simulated response

Illustrative only — a simplified model of tumor burden vs. anti-tumor immune activity over 12 weeks post-vaccination, driven by how many high-scoring targets you included.
