Glimpse. a world-model that learns by watching, not reading instructions

Below is a tiny toy inbox app. Flip to Observe and click around normally — every click teaches a small in-browser model what happens next. Flip to Imagine and the UI disappears: the model renders every next screen itself from what it has learned, frame by frame, with no access to the real app code. Thin coverage looks glitchy on purpose — that's the model improvising.
