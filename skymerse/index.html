Pick a flight, drag the ETA (or click the timeline), edit any NOTAM, METAR or TAF, or untick a NOTAM. Every message is parsed into structured effects, each runway end is checked against the aircraft's limits at the ETA, and NOTAMs are ranked by how much they actually move this flight's verdict instead of by publication order.

Rules this toy applies
  • A NOTAM applies when A) is the destination and B) ≤ ETA < C). Its plain-English line comes from rule-based parsing, standing in for a trained model.
  • Weather: the METAR if it was observed within 60 min of the ETA, otherwise the TAF (FM replaces, BECMG takes the worse value during its change window). TEMPO/PROB conditions that would fail a check make a usable runway marginal.
  • A runway end is suitable when it is open, LDA ≥ required (wet if precipitation), mean tailwind and crosswind are within limits (gusts over a limit = marginal), and an available approach has minima at or below the ceiling and visibility.
  • Toy minima: ILS 200 ft / 550 m, RNP 300 / 900, LOC 400 / 1000, VOR 500 / 1500. Approach lights out add 500 m.
  • Headings are treated as true. Destination only: no alternates, fuel or performance tables.

NOTAMs ranked by impact on this flight

Impact share = Shapley value over the runway NOTAMs in force: the engine reruns the whole runway check for every subset of them (2n runs) and averages each NOTAM's marginal effect on the verdict. "Without it" is plain leave-one-out, shown for comparison.
