Pod matcher: put idle interconnects to work

Stalled construction sites sit on grid power they cannot use while interest piles up. A small factory builds identical compute pods. Your job: decide which site gets the next pod. Cooling limits how much of each pod's rating survives local heat, so the best site is not always the one with the most megawatts. All sites and numbers are synthetic.
