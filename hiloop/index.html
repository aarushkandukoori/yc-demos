Campaign Bench · toy demo

Give it a task, a starting agent, and an eval. Watch it search for the fix.

This is a small working sketch of an "autoresearch" loop: it fans a task out across several improvement strategies at once, scores each one against a synthetic held-out eval, prunes the losers early, and reports back the single verified winner with a confidence interval — not just a leaderboard.

Baseline agent: gpt-class model + hand-written prompt, no tools.
More rounds = more pruning, sharper CI on the winner.
