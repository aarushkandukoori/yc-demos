Arc Planner

Toy point-to-point rocket hop solver. Pick a launch site and a target, load the payload, set the loft angle. It solves the ballistic burnout speed, checks it against a rocket-equation budget, and tests the arc against synthetic air-defense rings. All data is invented.

Model

Spherical Earth, vacuum Kepler arc from burnout to impact, no drag, instant burn. Range angle psi and loft angle gamma give the speed ratio nu = tan(psi/2) / (cos g (sin g + tan(psi/2) cos g)). Budget = Isp g ln(m0/mf) minus a fixed 450 m/s loss for gravity and drag. Vehicle numbers are made up.
