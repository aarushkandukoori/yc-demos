Spread vs. Depth

A toy model of a distributed field-data network. Hand out a limited pool of wearable capture rigs to local partners, run a collection week, push every clip through a quality gate, and see why hours spread over many workplaces can beat the same hours piled into one. All partners and numbers are synthetic.
