Multiply with adders: a log-domain toy

A multiplier grows with the square of its bit width; an adder grows linearly. If numbers live in the log domain, a * b becomes an integer add of two exponent codes. This page runs real dot products both ways, so you can see what the swap costs in accuracy and saves in silicon. Synthetic data, toy cost model.
