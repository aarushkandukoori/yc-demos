Touch Clock Lab

A camera and a tactile sensor never share a clock. Here a wearable force stream arrives late against an egocentric video. Slide the offset until the force onset lands on the frame where fingers first touch, or let cross-correlation find it. Everything below is synthetic and runs in your browser.
