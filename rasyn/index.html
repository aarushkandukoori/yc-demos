Blendbench / search a formulation spacetoy model, synthetic data

A mixture of ten ingredients has too many possible ratios to bench-test. Here a small surrogate model predicts five properties of any blend, with an uncertainty estimate, and a search loop hunts for blends that meet a spec. Mix by hand first, then press Search.

Bar = ensemble mean, black whisker = 1 std across 6 surrogate members, vertical tick = spec limit. A blend only counts as robust if it passes at mean ± 1 std.
