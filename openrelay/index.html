ScatterMesh — one API, any GPU on earth

A toy router that spreads inference jobs across a pool of mismatched GPUs — gaming cards, workstation cards, data-center accelerators, owned by nobody in particular — and picks a node per request the way a real hardware-agnostic inference layer would: by fit, cost, and who's actually free right now. Everything below runs client-side against synthetic nodes.
