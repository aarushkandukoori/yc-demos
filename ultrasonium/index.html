Heat-aware deposition controller

A toy model of one idea: building a part from solid feedstock is a control problem. Heat accumulates, thin features cook, and a fixed recipe drifts. Pick a part, then compare a fixed recipe against a controller that reads the heat field and corrects power cell by cell.

Each cell of the part is filled in raster order. The melt a cell receives is power times a heat-dependent gain, plus drift and noise. Too little leaves a void. Too much spills into neighboring empty cells. The controller estimates heat from a noisy sensor and trims power. Everything here is synthetic.

Independent demo built by Aarush Kandukoori, inspired by Ultrasonium (YC S26). Not affiliated with Ultrasonium.
