Storefront Concierge — toy demo

Every visitor to this vintage card shop gets their own shopping agent. Click around the catalog — the agent on the right watches what you look at and buy, builds a live memory of your taste, and re-sorts the shelf to match. This is a self-contained simulation: no backend, no network calls, just enough logic to show the mechanic.
