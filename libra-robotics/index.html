Crew Sim — three robots, one build, zero human choreography

A toy simulation of a coordinated robotic build crew. Three independent units — a placer, a feeder, and a fastener — negotiate a shared worksite in real time over a synthetic radio channel. No central script tells them what to do step by step; each bot reacts to what the others broadcast. Learned skills (alignment, fastening, handoff) get a little faster and more reliable each time the crew repeats them, and you can inject a fault mid-run to see the crew renegotiate around it.
