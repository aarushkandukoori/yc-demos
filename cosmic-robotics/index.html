PANELPLACER / toy install planner

One robot, one pallet of panels, a field of empty torque-tube slots. It plans a route, drives, fine-aligns against a tolerance, and places. Click any slot to drop a boulder there and watch it replan. Crank the wind to see lifts get held. All numbers are synthetic.
