Clinical RL Sandbox — a toy medical data-platform demo

This is a miniature illustration of one idea: turn bedside decisions into structured (state, action, reward, next-state) records that a model could actually train on. Play the clinician, act on a synthetic patient, and watch the transition dataset build itself on the right in real time. All patients and physiology here are fabricated for demonstration only.
