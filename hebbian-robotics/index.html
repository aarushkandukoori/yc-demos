Measure once, judge many

A toy quality-control pipeline for physical-AI data. Checks run once over a batch of robot episodes and record raw measurements — not pass/fail verdicts. Verdicts get computed afterward, live, by sliding a threshold. Change your mind about what counts as "bad" and every episode re-judges instantly, with zero reprocessing.

Notice the checks never re-run when you move a slider below. Measurements are evidence, thresholds are opinion — one pipeline run can serve a strict dataset and a lenient one, because the verdict is computed at query time, not baked into the data.
