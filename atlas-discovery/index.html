How the score works

For each drug, reversal = -cos(disease, drug) over the genes left on, each scaled by its pathway weight. Drugs that strongly push the apoptosis or mitochondria pathways upward take a safety penalty. This is a connectivity-map style heuristic on invented numbers, a demo of the mechanic and nothing more.
