LLoom

A toy demo of adaptive multi-model coordination: instead of sending every query to one big model, Loom scores task difficulty, routes it to a small team of specialist open-source models, and weaves their answers together. Inspired by Tracer (YC S26) — this is an independent, simplified illustration of the idea, not their product.
