Prefill small, decode large

A toy of cross-model KV cache transfer. A small model reads a long prompt, an adapter translates its cache into the big model's space, and the big model only decodes. The adapter is fitted live in your browser, and a held-out quality gate decides whether the transfer is allowed. All numbers are synthetic.

How the toy works

    
    
      
  1. Two fake models - both read the same hidden state. The small one gets an 8-number key per token, the large one a 16-number key with a nonlinear twist.
    2. 
      
  2. Calibrate - run both on a handful of samples, then solve a ridge regression that maps small keys to large keys.
    3. 
      
  3. Hold out - test the map on 200 samples it never saw. Training score alone would flatter it.
    4. 
      
  4. Gate - if held-out cosine clears your threshold the pair is allowed to transfer. Otherwise requests take the slow, safe path.
    5. 
      
  5. Check attention - one query attends over 48 cached tokens using the real large-model keys versus the translated ones. Similar bars mean similar outputs.
    6. 
    

    
Things to try - drag calibration samples down to 8 and watch the gate close, switch to the distant pair, or lower the gate and see a bad transfer sneak through.
