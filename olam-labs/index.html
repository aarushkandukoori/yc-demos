toy multi-agent arena

Coalition

Five rounds. A shared point pool each round. You and three AI agents each decide, independently, whether to cooperate or defect. The agents aren't scripted to a fixed pattern — each one tracks a running trust score toward you and toward the other agents, and that score shapes what it does next. Play it out, then look at the eval log the arena produces about each agent's behavior.
