Fetchpoint (concept demo)

Chat that goes fetch specific things, not just search

Type a research question. Fetchpoint fans it out across specialized connectors — professional profiles, filings, maps, live fares — instead of one blended web index, then hands the model back source-tagged facts to answer with.

Pick a sample query above (or write your own) and hit Run research.
