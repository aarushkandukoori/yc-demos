Docket Clear

A toy version of an agentic reviewer: pick a rubric, drop in an application, and watch the agent check each requirement against the document, cite its evidence, and hand a fully-audited file to a human for the final call. Everything below runs client-side against synthetic applications — nothing leaves your browser.

Backlog Queue

Select an application from the queue, or click "Run agents on entire queue" to review all of them in parallel.
