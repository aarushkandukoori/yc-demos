Titrate - a dose that follows the patient

Toy closed loop: a synthetic patient, a fixed one-size schedule, and a personalized schedule that adjusts every week from check-in data (weight change and side effects). Step week by week and watch the two paths diverge. Everything here is simulated, not medical advice.

1. Intake

The simulator keeps a hidden tolerance ceiling and response per patient. The personalized rules never see them, only the check-ins, like a real clinic.
