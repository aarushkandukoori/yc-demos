A toy of the ambient-listening idea: speech arrives in short chunks, and a tiny rule-based extractor turns casual talk into promises, deadlines and mood. Nothing leaves this page. All sample speech is synthetic.

Independent demo built by Aarush Kandukoori, inspired by Moonshot (YC S26). Not affiliated with Moonshot.
How it works: each chunk goes through (a) commitment patterns like "I'll / I promised / remind me / need to", (b) a person and deadline tagger, (c) closure detection ("sent it", "done with") that retires a loop, and (d) a word-list mood score. Everything shown is recomputed from the stored chunks, so deleting a chunk deletes whatever it caused. Click a loop to toggle it by hand.
