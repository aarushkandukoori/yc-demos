Circuit scheduler toy

An optical circuit switch connects 8 accelerators with light beams that can be re-pointed, but each re-pointing costs time. Pick a collective, let the controller plan a sequence of beam configurations, and compare it against a fixed ring of links. Everything here is simulated with synthetic numbers.

Click Plan + run controller. In manual mode, click a left port then a right port to patch a beam yourself; the demand it serves updates live.
