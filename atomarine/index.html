Sea Reactor Sim

A toy simulation of a marine-based reactor feeding a fleet of floating compute barges. Drag the demand and sea-state sliders and watch the reactor route power and seawater cooling across the fleet in real time — including what happens when one barge overheats and the dispatcher has to shed or shift load.
