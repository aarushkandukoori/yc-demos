Intake Triage Sandbox

A toy model of the safety screen that sits between a remote intake form and a clinician: answers go in, a transparent rule engine decides route (prescribe-eligible, needs clinician review, or refer out), and every decision shows which rule fired. All data is synthetic. Not medical advice.
