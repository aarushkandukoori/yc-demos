Episode Grader

A toy version of the idea behind grading robot behavior automatically: run a simulated pick-and-place episode, watch a rule-based evaluator judge success or failure the instant it ends, and compare its calls against synthetic human ground-truth labels across a batch. All physics, trajectories and labels below are fabricated in your browser — no backend, no real robot data.
