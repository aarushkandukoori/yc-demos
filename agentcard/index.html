Mandate Desk

A toy authorization engine for agent spending. Mint a card with a mandate (limit, merchant categories, transaction cap), then watch an "agent" try to check out. Every purchase runs through the same rule chain a real issuer would, and anything over your auto-approve line waits for a human. All data is synthetic.
