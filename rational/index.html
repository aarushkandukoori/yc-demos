Ledger Autopilot · AI bookkeeper demo

A toy version of the core mechanic behind an AI-run accounting firm: raw bank transactions come in, an AI agent categorizes each one against the chart of accounts with a confidence score and a written rationale, and the general ledger + P&L update live. Low-confidence calls get flagged for review instead of silently filed. Everything below runs in your browser — no data leaves this page.
