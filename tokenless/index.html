Gradeprobe

A toy reward-function auditor. A swarm of exploit strategies attacks a grader. If a strategy earns high reward while its true correctness is near zero, the grader has a reward hack. Patch the flaw, re-run, watch the hole close. All data is synthetic and everything runs in your browser.
