Lookahead Care Planner / toy

A synthetic member moves through health states each quarter. Pick which interventions are allowed, and a backward-search planner finds the action at every (quarter, state) that minimizes expected employer cost. Then 4,000 simulated members play out usual care versus the plan. All data is made up.
