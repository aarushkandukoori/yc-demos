Vision-Guided Rack Repair — toy demo

A miniature, synthetic simulation of a robot technician working a server rack: scan for faults, then use vision-guided alignment to grip a delicate component with sub-tolerance precision before executing the fix. Nothing here talks to real hardware — every rack, fault, and camera reading is generated in-browser.
