Zero-Shot Chemistry Sandbox

A toy illustration of extrapolative vs. interpolative molecule scoring. Every dot is a synthetic candidate compound plotted in a simplified 2D chemical-feature space. The grey cluster is "training data" the model has seen before. Somewhere outside that cluster sits a real binder for the target — one the model has never been trained on. Switch models and click candidates to see who finds it.
