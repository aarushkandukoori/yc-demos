Failure → Hypothesis → Recipe

A toy simulation of one idea: treat a model's failure mode as a data-collection hypothesis, run every incoming submission through a provenance-aware QA check (who, what device, what session, consent, rights — not just the file), and once enough submissions clear review, lock a reusable "recipe" you can scale up. Everything below runs in your browser on synthetic data — nothing is sent anywhere.
