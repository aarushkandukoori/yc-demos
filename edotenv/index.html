Alpha Decay Lab

A toy research loop: hypothesize a trading rule, backtest it against a synthetic market, then publish it. Publishing crowds the edge out of the market — the next research cycle is harder, and old rules stop working. Keep discovering to keep the loop alive. This is an independent, from-scratch homage to the idea of markets as a self-improving research environment — not the real product.

Hypothesis

Publishing decays the edge for this exact family in future cycles — that alpha is now crowded. Other families may open up as the regime shifts. This mirrors the core loop: form a hypothesis, test it, verify the result, and let the environment get harder in response to what you just learned.
