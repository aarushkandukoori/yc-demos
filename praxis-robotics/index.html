Clip feed (synthetic)

How it works: each tick, the scheduler picks an unmet cell weighted by remaining hours, then picks a worker whose site types include that environment. The worker records a clip with hand visibility, blur and stability scores drawn from their personal skill. Clips failing any gate threshold are rejected and do not count. Tight gates mean cleaner data but more rejections, longer runs and higher cost per accepted hour. A rare environment has few eligible workers, so it fills slowly. That is the trade-off this toy lets you feel.
