Constellation Coverage Sandbox

A miniature model of the core problem behind a distributed low-cost LEO comms network: how many satellites, in what orbit, does it take to keep a patch of ground continuously connected — without relying on any single country's ground infrastructure. Drop a target anywhere on the map, dial in the constellation, and watch coverage converge in real time.

Click or tap anywhere on the map to move the ground target. Simulated time runs ~450× real speed, so a 95-minute LEO orbit completes in about 12 seconds.

Continents are drawn schematically for orientation, not geodetically accurate. Orbital mechanics (Walker-delta planes, ground-track precession, elevation-mask footprint, two-way slant-range latency) are computed live in-browser from altitude and inclination — this is a simplified model for intuition, not a mission-design tool.
