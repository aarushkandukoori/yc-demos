Signal → Diagnosis → Fix → Verify

A minimal sandbox for the core loop behind autonomous facilities operations: stream in meter/equipment data, catch a drifting signal before it becomes a wasted bill, let the system propose a fix, approve it, and watch the fix get checked against real follow-up data instead of just being marked "done."

Operating loop

1

Detect

Scan meter + equipment signal for deviation from learned baseline.

2

Diagnose

Rank likely root causes against the site's equipment model.

3

Propose work order

Draft the fix, cost, and expected savings for human sign-off.

4

Verify

Re-pull meter data after the fix and confirm the savings actually landed.
