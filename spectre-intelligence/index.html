Evolutionary Trader Arena

A toy pod-shop. A population of AI traders competes on a synthetic price feed. Each generation, the losers get culled and the winners' strategies reproduce — with mutation — into the next round. No third-party training data: the fitness signal is P&L, generated by the traders' own decisions.
