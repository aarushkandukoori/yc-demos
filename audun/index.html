Ledgerhand

A toy plan negotiator. Pick an overdue account, read the strategy the engine builds from the profile, then play the debtor and watch it re-plan, concede, or hand off to a person. All data is synthetic and every rule runs in your browser.

offer >= floor  -> accept, schedule

                  conditional write-down
