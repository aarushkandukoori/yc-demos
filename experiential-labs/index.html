Tollgate

A single endpoint sits in front of every model you call. It routes each request, watches the traffic pile up, and — once there's enough of it — trains a smaller model on your own usage patterns that you own outright. Send a few requests below, then train it and watch the gateway shift traffic onto the cheaper model it just built.
