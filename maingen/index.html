Ops Desk Sandbox

A miniature stand-in for the kind of environment used to train agents that operate real infrastructure: a simulated solar-plus-battery site with live weather, price and grid-curtailment shocks. Dispatch the battery yourself, or flip on the autopilot policy and watch an agent trade against the same environment in real time.
