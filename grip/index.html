Grasp Ledger

A toy sorting line. Contaminants ride a belt of food scraps; a robot has three grip strategies and no idea which works on what. It learns from every success and failure. Watch the table turn from guesses into a policy.

Belt

Click any item on the belt to grab it by hand with the manual grip. Your attempts also train the model. Red-outlined items are contaminants; green-outlined ones are good organics and should stay.
