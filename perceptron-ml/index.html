Groundwork

a toy grounding engine for legal drafting

Pick a matter question below. An in-browser "drafting model" writes a memo with citations — then a separate verification pass checks each citation against a small mock source library before it's allowed to stay in the document. Nothing leaves your browser; all sources and facts here are synthetic.
