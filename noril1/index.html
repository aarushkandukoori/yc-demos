Waypoint Bench

Teach a toy two-arm robot by showing it once. Drag the hands to demonstrate, record keyframes, replay them, then shuffle the blocks and see why a replay of raw coordinates breaks while object-relative keyframes keep working.

Workcell (front view)

Drag in the workcell to move a hand. Goal: get all four blocks into the tray.
Arm
Keys: 1 left grip, 2 right grip, R record, Enter play, S shuffle. Auto arm = the side of the cell you click on.
