Wetware Trainer — a toy neuron culture that learns next-token prediction

A simulated dish of 64 cultured neurons. Each token in a made-up sequence gets converted to an electrical stimulation pattern across the dish. The culture predicts what comes next; right guesses reinforce the synapses that fired, wrong guesses weaken them. Train it enough and it starts to learn the sequence's structure — with no GPU involved, just a plausible simulation of Hebbian-style reinforcement.
