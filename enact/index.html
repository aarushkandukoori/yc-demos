Failure Loop

A toy simulator for one idea: robot policies get reliable not from more random data, but from data targeted at their actual failure modes. Roll out a policy, click a failure to inspect it, collect a recovery demo, retrain, and watch the success rate move — only on the failures you actually addressed.
