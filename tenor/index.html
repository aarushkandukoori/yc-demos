Loopwright

A toy closed loop for an AI sales-ops agent. Every lead is handled by one cycle: gather context, decide under policy, act, observe the outcome, and update what it believes. Flip the governance rules and watch the ledger and the learned table change. All data here is synthetic and the "agent" is about 60 lines of JS.
