Morning Queue

A toy of one idea: rank a pool of local prospects against your business, write each opener from that lead's strongest signal, and stop at an approval gate. Tweak the profile and weights, watch the queue re-rank and the drafts rewrite. Nothing is ever sent. All data is synthetic.
