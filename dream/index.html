ScanGate

A toy gate-scan simulator. Drive an asset through the gate on intake, then again on return — ScanGate diffs the two 360° captures and flags anything new. This is an original demo inspired by pocket AI damage cameras, built to explore the core mechanic: automatic before/after diffing of a physical asset.
