One Demonstration, Any Part Position

Drag the part into the bin once — that's your only training example. Then scatter new parts anywhere on the line, at any angle, and watch a learned pick-and-place policy generalize the single demo instead of failing like a hard-coded coordinate program would.

Drag the teal part into the "bin" zone to record your one demonstration.

How this mimics the real thing

  • 1
    Show it once — drag the part to where it belongs. That relative motion (start pose → place pose) is the entire training set.
  • 2
    New parts land anywhere on the line, rotated differently, like a real high-mix cell.
  • 3
    The policy re-derives the same relative grasp-and-place transform for each new pose, instead of replaying fixed coordinates.
  • 4
    Compare against a hard-coded program, which only ever reaches for the original spot.
